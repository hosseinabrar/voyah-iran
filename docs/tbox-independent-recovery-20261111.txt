or change protection to force entry. OEM PBL remains unavailable for audit.

Offline relocated-symbol disassembly of our live spidev.ko shows spidev_write
at 0xce4 polls slave-ready at 0xf2c..0xf3c, retries at 0x1070..0x1098,
then returns -EIO at 0x107c before the spi sync call at 0x1014. No recovery
GPIO write occurs in that not-ready path. This corroborates that failed
writes cannot establish that any message reached MCU, much less CAN/BCM.
No GPIO toggles, driver reload, session changes, or flash performed.

SPI edge recovery trial (explicit owner authorization)
Mapped GPIO361 (physical93) as MPU output slave_tx_en, and 349/359
as MCU-driven inputs. spidev_read_work sets output high at0x274,
low at0x2e4/0x358. Applied bounded20ms and200ms high pulses with
SPI0 temporarily unbound and vnsd stopped. Both inputs remained0
throughout sampled intervals; no readiness or MCU response observed.
Restored output low, unexported pins, rebound driver and restarted vnsd.

IMPORTANT adverse result: the restart then caused Linux kernel panic.
pstore identifies spi_slave_abort+0x18 called by spidev_release+0x160.
The OEM release path is not safe after unbind with an old open descriptor.
This was caused by this diagnostic sequence, not evidence of an MCU reboot.
Do not repeat unbind/rebind with open descriptors. Host trial script now
hard-disabled as evidence; no persistent driver/firmware modification.
